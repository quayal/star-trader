# R23 — A ship must be present at a system to trade there

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: asked whether the Ship-jump phase's initiative-set resolution order actually affects anything, since jumps are per-ship actions with no stated shared resource. The answer revealed the real reason ships exist in v1 at all: "the only reason to have ships is to make use of mechanism for storing goods and sell on cap. And the ability to sell goods in system (need to have ship there)." Also asked whether buying has the same requirement.
- Decision and why: a ship must be physically present at a system to trade there. A sell draws from that ship's held cargo (capped by R10). A buy's goods go into a present ship's cargo (capped by R16, overflow lost). If the player has no ship at the system at all, bought goods are simply lost entirely — consistent with R16's "overflow is lost" pattern, just with zero capacity. Since Ship-jumps now resolve before Trading (corrected alongside this decision — see R19/R20's record), ship positions are always settled by the time Trading checks them.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this is what makes ships meaningful at all for v1 — otherwise they'd be inert flavor. The exact capacity numbers still depend on Q03/Q06.
- Status: active
