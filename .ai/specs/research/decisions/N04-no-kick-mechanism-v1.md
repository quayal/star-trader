# N04 — No mechanism to remove or replace an absent player in v1

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: Q02's follow-up asked whether v1 needs any way to remove or replace a long-absent player (vote-kick, host action, bot takeover), given D05 (indefinite resume) and D06 (pause after repeated absence).
- Decision and why: the creator chose to leave this out of v1. An absent player's seat simply waits; no removal, replacement, or takeover mechanism exists yet.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this is the third piece of the stall risk in A06 — together with D05 and D06, nothing stops an absent player from freezing a game indefinitely. Accepted as a known risk, not resolved. Revisit if real play shows this happens often enough that a kick or bot-takeover mechanism becomes necessary.
