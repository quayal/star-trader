# R15 — No bid/ask price cap; extreme bids saturate rather than crash or grow unbounded

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: `SDCalculator`'s indicator↔modifier table only covers modifier values -6 to +7. `TransactionAmountCalculator` looks up a winning offer's price-vs-current-price difference directly in that table; a difference outside that range finds no match and crashes (`.min()`/`.max()` on an empty list) — a real, reachable bug given the legal 1–20 global price bounds. Initially proposed as a hard cap on what price could even be offered (current price ±6/+7); the creator corrected this: the offer price itself should be uncapped (so it can still win an auction), only its *effect* should be bounded.
- Decision and why: an offer's price has no cap beyond the existing 1–20 global bounds, and any price in that range can win the auction (`BestOfferCalculator` compares raw prices, unaffected). But when computing R13's amount ceiling and R14's reference-price move, an offer priced more than 7 above (buy) or 6 below (sell) the current price is treated as pegged to the extreme indicator (-18 or +18) rather than its literal price. This closes the crash risk entirely — the lookup always resolves to a defined indicator — while also naturally capping both the amount ceiling and the reference-price move at the table's edges (+7 buy / -6 sell).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this is a pure resolution-time clamp, not a submission-time restriction; no change needed to `TradeDesk.submit`'s price validation. Revisit only if real play shows the ±6/+7 saturation point feels wrong.
- Status: active
