# R21 — Turn initiative is an all-pay money auction, resolved with a d2 roll

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: Q05 (from the turn-taking session) asked how a turn's initiative holder is determined. Asked what currency is bid, and whether only the winner pays or everyone who bids.
- Decision and why: during Bidding, a player may place a money bid of 0 or more (capped by what they currently have) to compete for initiative. Every bidder pays their own bid regardless of outcome — an all-pay auction, not a winner-pays auction. The winner is whoever has the highest bid plus a d2 roll; ties are broken by rerolling until resolved.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this is a real money sink separate from trading (R10/R12), and resolves before Trading in sequence (Key flow step 7), so a large initiative bid genuinely reduces what's left to spend in that turn's Trading phase. Revisit if Q06's actual starting-balance numbers make this feel too punishing or trivial in practice.
