# R16 — Buying is capped only by money; storage overflow is lost, not blocked

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: R10 capped a SELL by ship/warehouse holdings. Asked by symmetry whether a BUY should likewise be capped by remaining storage space, in addition to R12's affordability cap.
- Decision and why: the creator chose not to cap buying by storage. A buy is capped only by money (R12); a player may legally buy more than their ship/warehouse can currently hold, and the quantity beyond capacity is simply lost — not refunded, and does not block or reduce the purchase itself.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the player still pays full price for the lost portion, making over-buying a real (if avoidable) waste rather than a blocked action — a deliberate asymmetry with selling. The exact capacity numbers this cap would trigger against remain open (Q03); the rule itself (overflow is lost) does not depend on those numbers.
- Status: active
