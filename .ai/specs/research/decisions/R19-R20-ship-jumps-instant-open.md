# R19/R20 — Ship jumps are instant; destination is chosen openly during the Ship-jump phase

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: `ships.html` shows fuel, hull integrity, repair, and multi-cycle transit (ETA) — asked how much of that is needed for a super-basic MVP. First draft also had the jump destination declared secretly during Bidding, mirroring trade offers.
- Decision and why: ship jumps are instant for v1 — no fuel, hull, repair, or transit time; a ship just relocates to its destination when the Ship-jump phase resolves (R19). Range is unrestricted — any of the 6 systems, regardless of current location. The destination itself is NOT declared secretly during Bidding (the session's first draft): it's chosen openly during the Ship-jump phase itself, one player at a time, in the order the turn's initiative holder (R21) sets (R20) — corrected after discussing what the initiative-set order actually does, which also surfaced R23 (a ship must be present to trade).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this correction happened before anything was committed, so no superseding entry was needed — the file simply reflects the corrected version. `N07` names the dropped mockup depth explicitly.
- Status: active
