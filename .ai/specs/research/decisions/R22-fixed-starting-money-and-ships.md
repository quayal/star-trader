# R22 — Fixed starting money and 3 ships; no purchasing for v1

- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator
- Context and the options weighed: asked whether money moves anywhere beyond trading (R10/R12) for v1 — e.g. starting balance, ship purchase cost, per-turn fees.
- Decision and why: each player starts the game with a fixed money balance and exactly 3 ships, each with a predetermined cargo capacity. Ships are not purchased, built, or commissioned for v1 — directly stated purpose: "this is only to playtest the login, turns, trading and visuals."
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the actual starting balance and the 3 ships' capacities are not yet numbered — tracked as Q06, which also feeds Q03 (the broader ship/warehouse inventory model). N07 names the dropped ship-purchase/fuel/hull depth explicitly.
