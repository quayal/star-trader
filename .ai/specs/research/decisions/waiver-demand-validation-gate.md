- Date, owner: 2026-10-20, creator (maintainer)
- Context: an `om-backlog --dry-run` on `product-brief.md` stopped at step 1 — Problems and Target group carry `[ASSUMPTION]` tags (no external demand validation sought), which triggers the research-variant backlog (a 2-task "find out if anyone wants this" epic) instead of filing the Scope/Now implementation epics.
- Decision and why: the creator, as maintainer and sole decider on this pre-launch solo project, waives the evidentiary-readiness gate. Demand validation was deliberately never sought — it's an own idea built for the creator/friends, not a case of missing evidence that should block implementation. `SDLC.md`'s Definition of Ready explicitly allows a maintainer to waive an item by saying so.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: this waiver covers evidentiary readiness only. It does not waive `active`-status requirements on individual Decisions/Rules, and open questions (Q01, Q03/Q06, Q04, Q07) remain in force per their own blocking/non-blocking status. Revisit if the project ever needs to demonstrate real demand (e.g. before a public launch).
