# Sabotage

**Brief:** `.ai/specs/product-brief.md` — R29 (money is private), R46–R47 (factories, Collect), R50 (warehouses and
factories are public), R76 (hits), R79 (repair), R81 (criminal connections have no effect yet), R85–R86 (reputation).
**Source:** the creator's vault note `10-projects/Trader/Operatives and sabotage.md` (the Sabotage table) and their answers of
2026-11-20, including the same day's refinements: the factory size modifies only the effect roll; the saboteur picks who is
blamed and which goods go missing; sabotage happens in the Opportunity phase; and a ship's hits and cargo are handled as in
battle.
**Depends on:** `2026-11-20-opportunity-phase.md`.
**Related:** agents (spec to come). Shorty, Silent and JB change sabotage; see "Hooks for agents".
**Status:** proposed, not started.

## 📝 TLDR

In the **Opportunity** phase a player may pay **20 HT × the target system's legal system** to sabotage another player's
**ship in Port**, **warehouse** or **factory**. They make two rolls of 2d6:
- The **effect roll** adds their criminal connections and, against a factory, subtracts its units. It gives the hits on the
  ship, the units missing from the warehouse (chosen by the saboteur), or the turns the factory produces nothing.
- The **reputation roll** adds only their criminal connections. It gives how much reputation the saboteur loses.

On the highest row (21+) the saboteur loses nothing and an **innocent player of their choosing** is blamed and loses 8.

## 📝 Problem Statement

The game has no indirect way to hurt a rival. Battle needs ships in Space at the same system, and then the attacker pays
for it in reputation. Sabotage adds that indirect pressure, paid for in money and the risk of exposure, and finally gives
criminal connections something to do. Better connections hit harder and are caught less often. A large factory is harder
to damage, but no harder to get away with.

## 📋 Acceptance criteria

### Ordering a sabotage
1. **When:** on the saboteur's Opportunity turn (players act in turn order), after their Collect. **At most once per
   Opportunity** per player. It doesn't need
   a ship or anything else at the target's system.
2. **Targets:** another player's
   - **ship in Port** at any system. Ships in the yard, on a Planet or in Space can't be targeted.
   - **warehouse** at any system.
   - **factory** at any system.

   A player can't sabotage their own assets.
3. **Who gets blamed:** the saboteur names an **innocent player** in the same order, to be blamed if the reputation roll
   is 21+. It can be anyone except themselves and the target. In a two-player game nobody else is left, so the target is
   named automatically.
4. **Cost:** 20 HT × the legal system of the target's star system (`LocationStats.legalSystem`; Gamma Leporis, legal
   system 3, costs 60 HT). It's paid up front whatever the outcome, and refused if the player can't pay:
   `Sabotage at Gamma Leporis costs 60 HT; you have 45 HT.` The cost is private (R29). Loans are taken only in Investment,
   so a player can't borrow to sabotage.
5. Before confirming, the form on Infrastructure shows the target (*player → system → ship / warehouse / factory*), the
   innocent player, the cost, and both modifiers: `Effect +3 criminal −4 factory units`, `Reputation +3 criminal`.

### The rolls
6. The server makes **two rolls of 2d6** at once:
   - **Effect roll:** 2d6 + criminal connections − the target factory's units (factory targets only).
   - **Reputation roll:** 2d6 + criminal connections. A factory's size never changes it.
7. Each roll is read against the creator's table, the effect roll in the Effect column and the reputation roll in the
   Reputation loss column. A total below 2 (a large factory) reads as the 2–6 row.

   | 2d6 + modifiers | Effect | Reputation loss |
   |---|---|---|
   | 2–6 (or less) | 0 | 0 |
   | 7–10 | 0 | 10 |
   | 11–12 | 1 | 10 |
   | 13–14 | 2 | 6 |
   | 15–16 | 3 | 4 |
   | 17–18 | 5 | 0 |
   | 19–20 | 7 | 0 |
   | 21+ | 10 | the innocent player: 8 |

### Effects (effect roll)
8. **Ship in Port:** the ship takes that many **hits**, distributed as in battle (R76): its **owner** places them on the
   ship's card on Fleet. A module is damaged by 1 hit and destroyed by 2, the hull takes up to its shields, and one more
   destroys the ship.
   - **Cargo that no longer fits** after the damage goes to the owner's warehouse at that system as far as it fits. The
     owner chooses what goes there and what's lost (R76). Nothing can be bought to save it, because warehouses are bought
     only in Investment.
   - A ship destroyed this way goes with its cargo (R77).
   - The owner places them at once, from a notification, even if it isn't their Opportunity turn. Opportunity doesn't
     close into Investment until they have. Repair waits for Investment (R79).
9. **Warehouse:** the effect is the number of **units missing**, and the **saboteur chooses them**. After the roll the
   saboteur is shown that warehouse's contents (the only time they're revealed to another player) and picks the units
   (`3 ISO, 2 FOOD`). A warehouse holding no more than the effect loses everything, with no choice needed. The saboteur
   can't signal done until they have chosen.
10. **Factory:** the effect is the number of **turns disabled**: the factory's next N Collects produce nothing. If the owner
    comes later in the turn order and hasn't collected yet, this turn's Collect is the first of them. A second sabotage on a disabled factory
    adds its turns. Infrastructure and Corporations show `disabled, 2 more turns`.
11. **Effect 0:** nothing happens to the target, and the saboteur's cost is still spent.

### Reputation (reputation roll)
12. The saboteur loses the amount the table gives (R86 gets a new reason), logged like every reputation change:
    `Vega Lines's reputation: −6 for sabotage (rolled 2d6+3: 14) → 14`. A loss of 0 isn't logged.
13. **21+:** the saboteur loses nothing. The innocent player they named loses 8:
    `Kessler Cargo's reputation: −8, blamed for sabotage → 12`.

### What everyone sees
14. **Public:** one log line per sabotage with an effect, naming the target and the effect but not the saboteur:
    - `Sabotage at Tau Ceti: Orion Freight's Starfarer took 3 hits`
    - `Sabotage at Tau Ceti: Orion Freight's warehouse lost 5 u`
    - `Sabotage at Mu Herculis: Orion Freight's CHIPS factory disabled for 2 turns`

    A sabotage with effect 0 isn't logged. Only the reputation lines (criteria 12–13) point at anyone.
15. **Private to the saboteur:** both rolls and their totals, the effect and the reputation result, in a toast and on their
    Infrastructure, plus the target warehouse's contents while they choose.
16. **Private to the target:** which goods went missing, and which cargo was lost from a damaged ship. A notification
    (pop-up and card) tells them what happened, and for a ship it asks them to place the hits.

### Hooks for agents (built with the agents spec, not here)
- **Shorty:** +4 on both rolls if Shorty is present at the target's system.
- **Silent:** in Port at a system, makes all of their owner's assets there immune. Agents are secret, so the attempt is
  paid for and then fails: no rolls, no effect, no reputation loss, and the log reveals Silent (see the agents spec).
- **JB:** every reputation loss of JB's owner is one smaller, the sabotage loss and the blame included.

## 📝 Proposed Solution

A `Sabotage` value object resolves one attempt. It reads the creator's table (`SabotageTable`) and owns both modifiers, both
rolls and the blame. `Game.sabotage(...)` checks the phase and the once-per-Opportunity rule, has the saboteur pay, asks the
target's owning class for the effect, then applies the reputation changes and writes the log:
- `Ship.withPendingHits` reuses the battle's hit placement, followed by its cargo-overflow choice.
- `Warehouse` gets a pending loss for the saboteur to choose.
- `Factory.disabledFor`.

Two effects wait on a player's choice: the hits on a ship wait for its owner, and the goods missing from a warehouse wait
for the saboteur. The saboteur's choice is part of their own turn, so they can't finish it until they've made it. The ship owner's choice is
made out of turn, and Opportunity won't close until it's made (opportunity spec, criterion 4).

**Alternative considered:** random warehouse losses. Superseded by the creator's ruling that the saboteur chooses.

## 📝 Architecture and data model

- `game/Sabotage.kt`, `game/SabotageTable.kt` (new): the table, as the creator wrote it. `SabotageTarget` (sealed: ship by
  name, warehouse by system, factory by system + goods).
- `Factory.disabledTurns: Int`. Collect treats a disabled factory as producing 0, and each Collect counts down by 1.
- `Warehouse.withoutUnits(lots)`. `Turn.pendingWarehouseLosses` (saboteur, target warehouse, count), resolved by the
  saboteur's choice.
- `Ship`: pending sabotage hits reuse the battle's `HitPlacement` and overflow-to-warehouse path, outside a battle.
- `Turn.sabotagedBy: List<String>`, emptied with Opportunity.
- `Player.loseReputation(…)`: the new reasons `sabotage` and `blamed for sabotage`.
- Web: `POST /api/games/{id}/sabotage` (`target`, `blamed`), `POST …/sabotage/losses` (the saboteur's choice), and the
  existing hit-placement endpoint opened to Opportunity. `FactoryResponseDto.disabledTurns`. The saboteur's view carries
  the target warehouse's contents only while a choice is pending.
- DynamoDB: new attributes with defaults, no migration.
- Frontend: a Sabotage card on Infrastructure (target, innocent player, cost, modifiers, result, then the loss picker), hit
  placement on Fleet outside battle, and the disabled badge on factories.

## 📝 Edge Cases

- **A target ship that leaves Port first:** it can't happen, because ships don't move in Opportunity. A ship still in the
  yard can't be targeted: it leaves the yard in Investment (R63).
- **A ship with too few working parts to absorb the hits** is destroyed (R76).
- **A warehouse emptied earlier in the same phase:** the effect finds nothing, so there's no log line and no choice.
- **A factory sold or extended later:** the remaining disabled turns stay with the factory. Selling it all (R96) removes
  them.
- **Last turn:** sabotage is allowed. A factory disabled past the end of the game doesn't matter.
- **Reputation clamping:** a −10 at reputation 4 is logged `→ 0` (R85).

## Defaults I chose (override any)

- At most **once per Opportunity** per player. No presence at the target is needed.
- A ship can be sabotaged only **in Port** (not in the yard, on a Planet or in Space).
- The cost is the legal system of the **target's** system.
- The innocent player is named **with the order**, before the rolls. In a two-player game it's the target.
- The saboteur **sees the target warehouse's contents** only to choose the missing units.
- **Disabled turns add up** across sabotages.
- **The saboteur is never named** in the sabotage line. Effect-0 attempts aren't logged.
