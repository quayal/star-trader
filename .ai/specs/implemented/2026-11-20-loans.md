# Loans

**Brief:** `.ai/specs/product-brief.md` — N05 (loans out of scope; superseded here, D22), D21, R29 (money is private),
R39 (most money wins), R44 (Investment), R66 (selling a ship), R85–R86 (reputation, the deferred −4 for unpaid interest).
**Source:** the creator's answers of 2026-11-20, including two corrections the same day: interest is at least 1 HT,
and factories can be sold for 5 × the good's price per unit.
**Status:** proposed, not started.

## 📝 TLDR

A player can borrow money in Investment for **4 or 8 turns**, with one loan open at a time and up to
**100 HT × ships + 20 HT × warehouse blocks + cash**. The **interest** is fixed when the loan is taken: (loan − reputation) / 20
per turn for a 4-turn loan, and (loan − reputation) / 10 for an 8-turn loan. It falls due in every Investment of the term.
**Unpaid interest** costs 4 reputation and is added to the loan, while the interest itself stays the same. The loan can be
repaid in full in any Investment. A loan **still open when its term ends** is collected: from cash first, then by selling
the borrower's ships and then factory units. **Factories can now be sold** in any Investment for 5 × the good's price
per unit. **At game end** an open loan counts against the borrower's money.

## 📝 Problem Statement

Today the only money is what a player has earned, so a good opportunity early on (a factory, a second ship) can be out of
reach. In the board game, loans let a player buy growth now and pay for it over time. The cost is interest, which is
cheaper with a good reputation, and the risk is losing assets if they can't pay. Reputation (R85–R88) already exists and
keeps a slot for the unpaid-interest penalty (R86, deferred). This spec builds that penalty.

## 📋 Acceptance criteria

### Taking a loan
1. **When:** in Investment, on Infrastructure, by a player who has no open loan. It doesn't wait for Collect, because
   borrowing doesn't use output.
2. **Amount and term:** a whole number of HT from 1 up to the **cap**, and a term of **4 or 8 turns**.
   - **Cap** = 100 × ships the player owns (ships in the yard and damaged ships included) + 20 × all their warehouse blocks +
     their cash at that moment. It's shown beside the form.
   - Refused above the cap: `You can borrow at most 340 HT.`
   - Refused while a loan is open: `You already have a loan; repay it before taking another.`
3. **Interest per turn**, fixed when the loan is taken and never recalculated:
   - 4-turn loan: ⌊(amount − reputation) / 20⌋
   - 8-turn loan: ⌊(amount − reputation) / 10⌋
   - **At least 1 HT** (creator, 2026-11-20). The form shows the interest for both terms before the player confirms.
   - Example: 300 HT at reputation 20 costs 14 HT a turn over 4 turns, or 28 HT a turn over 8 turns.
4. The amount is paid into the player's cash at once and can be spent in the same Investment.

### Interest
5. **When it's due:** in every Investment of the term, starting the turn after the loan is taken. A loan taken in turn N
   has interest due in turns N+1 … N+term, so 4 or 8 payments.
6. **Paying:** a "Pay interest (14 HT)" action on Infrastructure, once per Investment while it's due. Refused if the player
   can't afford it: `Interest is 14 HT; you have 9 HT.` The payment is private: it isn't logged (R29).
7. **Not paying:** when Investment closes, a player whose interest was due and unpaid
   - loses 4 reputation, with a public log line that doesn't give the amount:
     `Vega Lines didn't pay loan interest: reputation −4 → 16`
   - has that turn's interest added to the loan's outstanding amount. The interest per turn doesn't change (creator: "no
     change on interest payment").
8. **The reminder:** while interest is due and unpaid, Infrastructure shows it and the "Done investing" buttons (Infrastructure
   and the Deck's top bar) warn before signalling done: `Your loan interest (14 HT) is unpaid: −4 reputation, and it's
   added to your loan.`

### Repaying early
9. In any Investment, including the one the loan was taken in, the player may **repay the whole outstanding amount**. If that
   turn's interest is due and unpaid, it's paid as part of the repayment, so the action costs outstanding amount + interest.
   Refused if they can't afford it all. The loan closes, so the player may take a new one in a later Investment. Partial
   repayment isn't offered. It's private, not logged.

### End of the term
10. **When the loan is due:** when the Investment of its last interest turn closes (turn N+term), after the unpaid-interest
    penalty (criterion 7), the outstanding amount is collected:
    1. From cash, as far as it goes.
    2. If that's not enough, the borrower's **ships are seized**: sold at their R66 price (half hull + modules, rounded
       down), highest sale value first, until the debt is covered or no ships are left. Each ship's cargo goes to the
       borrower's warehouse at its system as far as it fits, and the rest is lost (R66).
    3. If that's still not enough, **factory units are seized** at their sale price (criterion 16), from the factory with the
       highest unit price first, only as many units as the debt needs. A factory left with no units is removed.
    4. Any surplus from the last ship or units sold goes into cash. Anything still owed after that is **written off**:
       cash is 0 and the loan closes.
11. The public log names each seizure without amounts: `Vega Lines's loan fell due: Starfarer and 3 u of the ISO factory at
    Tau Ceti seized`. A loan fully repaid
    from cash at the end of its term isn't logged. The borrower sees the collected amount privately.
12. To avoid surprises, a loan due this Investment is flagged on Infrastructure: `Your loan (312 HT) falls due when this
    Investment closes.`

### Game end
13. **Final money** = cash − outstanding amount of any open loan, and it can be negative. Standings, winners and the final
    money shown to everyone (R39) all use it. An open loan is never collected at game end: no ships are seized, it's only
    subtracted.
14. A loan may be taken even if its term runs past the last turn. Criterion 13 covers it.

### Selling factories
16. **In any Investment** (Collect is already done, in Opportunity), a player may sell any number of units
    of one of their factories for **5 × the good's price per unit**. The price is the good's current price at the factory's
    own system. Every system where a good can be built also trades it (R46), so there's always one. Selling every unit removes the factory, and
    the player may build it again later (R46). Selling is applied at once, with no limit per turn.
17. The sale is logged publicly without the amount, like a ship sale (R66): `Vega Lines sold 2 u of its ISO factory at Tau
    Ceti`. The money is private (R29).
18. On Infrastructure, each of the player's factories gets a Sell action with a unit count and the price shown before
    confirming.

### Visibility
15. A loan is **private**, like money (R29): its existence, amount, term and interest are visible only to the borrower. On
    Infrastructure the borrower sees: outstanding amount, interest per turn, turns left, and whether this turn's interest
    is paid. Other players learn about a loan only from the public lines (criteria 7 and 11). The Assets tab shows the open
    loan as a liability under money.

## 📝 Proposed Solution

A `Loan` value owned by `Player` holds the outstanding amount, the fixed interest, the turn it was taken and its term. `Loan`
owns the interest formula, the turns it's due and when it falls due. `Player` owns the cap, taking, paying, repaying and
seizure, with intent-named methods. `Game` coordinates at Investment close: it calls the per-player penalty and collection
and writes the log lines. The interest-paid flag lives on `Turn` and is emptied with Investment, like `reputationBought`.

**Alternatives considered:**
- *Paying interest automatically:* rejected, because the creator's rule makes not paying a choice with a penalty, and an
  automatic payment would remove it.
- *Seizing warehouses as well:* rejected, because warehouses have no sale price. Debt left after ships and factories is
  written off instead.

## 📝 Architecture and data model

- `game/Loan.kt` (new): `amount` (outstanding), `interest`, `takenInTurn`, `term` (`LoanTerm` enum: `FOUR`, `EIGHT` with
  divisor 20 and 10). `Loan.take(amount, term, reputation, turn)`, `isInterestDue(turn)`, `fallsDueIn(turn)`,
  `withInterestAdded()`, `repayment(interestDue)`.
- `Player.loan: Loan?`, plus `borrowingCap()`, `takeLoan(...)`, `payInterest()`, `repayLoan(...)`, `withUnpaidInterest()`
  (−4 reputation + interest added), `withLoanCollected()` (cash, then ships by `saleValue()`, rest written off),
  `finalMoney()`.
- `Factory.saleValuePerUnit(prices)` (5 × the price at its system) and `withoutUnits(n)`. `Player.sellFactoryUnits(...)` removes an
  emptied factory.
- `Turn.interestPaid: List<String>`, emptied with Investment.
- `Game`: `takeLoan`, `payInterest`, `repayLoan` (each `requireInvesting`). At Investment close, before `endTurn`'s
  reputation recovery: unpaid-interest penalties, then loans falling due, both logged. `standings()`/`winners()` sort by
  `finalMoney()`.
- `Game.sellFactoryUnits` (`requireInvesting`, after Collect), logged. `POST /api/games/{id}/factories/sell` (`system`, `goods`,
  `units`).
- `InvestmentService`: four methods (loan take/interest/repay, factory sale), one transaction each. `InvalidInvestmentException` for refusals.
- Web: `POST /api/games/{id}/loan` (`amount`, `term`), `POST …/loan/interest`, `POST …/loan/repay`. `PlayerView` carries
  `loan` only for the viewer (A05). The final result DTO uses final money.
- DynamoDB: a nullable `loan` map on `PlayerItem`, no migration.
- Frontend: a Loan card on Infrastructure (take form with live cap and both interests; open-loan state with Pay interest
  and Repay), the done-investing warning, and a liability line on Assets.

## 📝 Edge Cases

- **Reputation at or above the amount** (borrowing 15 at reputation 20): the formula gives 0 or less, so the minimum of
  1 HT applies.
- **Reputation at 0 or 40 when the penalty hits:** clamped as usual (R85), and the line still shows −4.
- **Taking a loan in the last Investment of the game:** it's subtracted at once (criterion 13), so it's harmless.
- **A ship seized while it's in the yard** (bought last turn): it's sold like any other ship. It has no cargo.
- **The only ship is seized:** allowed, because R66 lets a player sell every ship.
- **The cap uses cash at the moment of borrowing**, so spending first and then borrowing gives a smaller cap. That's
  intended: the cap reflects what the player has now.

## Defaults I chose (override any)

- Interest is rounded **down**, then raised to the 1 HT minimum.
- Loans can be taken only in **Investment** and from Infrastructure.
- Interest is paid by **an explicit action**, not automatically, with a warning when signalling done.
- Interest is first due **the turn after** the loan is taken, and there are exactly *term* payments.
- Early repayment covers the **whole** amount plus any unpaid interest for that turn. No partial repayment.
- Seizure takes **ships first** (highest sale value first), **then factory units** (highest unit price first). Any
  remaining debt is written off with no further penalty.
- Factory units can be sold **partly**, any number at a time.
- Loans are **private**. Only the penalty and seizure lines are public.
