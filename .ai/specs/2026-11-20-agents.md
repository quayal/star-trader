# Agents (special operatives)

**Brief:** `.ai/specs/product-brief.md` — R02 and R06 (confidential advantages, acquired during play and shareable), A05
(authorizing confidential reads), R27 (initiative roll), R30 (Port, Planet, Space), R37–R38 (Wholesaler, Dictator), R68
(jump chance), R70–R71 (interception), R75 (salvo), R77 (destroyed ships), R85–R86 (reputation), R97–R101 (sabotage).
**Source:** the creator's vault note `10-projects/Trader/Operatives and sabotage.md` (the Agents table) and their answers of
2026-11-20.
**Depends on:** `2026-11-20-opportunity-phase.md`, `2026-11-20-sabotage.md` (Shorty, Silent, JB).
**Status:** proposed, not started.

## 📝 TLDR

Ten named agents, each **unique** in the game, can be **recruited** in the Opportunity phase. The player pays an amount they
choose, then rolls 2d6 × 5; if the roll is at or below what they paid, a **random** agent joins them. Paying 60 HT always
works. Agents travel **aboard their owner's ships** or wait **on their own in a Port or on a Planet**. They're moved and can
be **sold to another player** in Opportunity.
- **What others see:** that a player has an agent at a place, never **which** one. An agent's identity shows only in the log
  line of the moment it **acts** (its effect applies), and nothing tracks it afterwards. Two agents of the same player who
  meet are indistinguishable again.
- **How agents die:** when their ship is destroyed in Space.

## 📝 Problem Statement

R02 and R06 have promised confidential, tradeable advantages since the start, and agents are the first. They add hidden
information, an investment that can be lost, and a reason to move ships for something other than cargo. Most of their
effects plug into rules that already exist: jumps, initiative, interception, battle, trading, sabotage and reputation.

## 📋 Acceptance criteria

### The agents
1. **The pool** is the creator's ten agents, each one copy per game. Each effect applies automatically whenever its condition
   holds, and each time it does, the agent **acts**. "Present at a system" means anywhere there: Port, Planet, or aboard a
   ship in any of the three.

   | Agent | Effect | Hooks into |
   |---|---|---|
   | Novak | +4 to the jump result of the ship he travels on | R68 jump chance |
   | Badger | −3 to the security of a planet a ship carrying him tries to land on | planet landing (doesn't exist yet) |
   | Wolf | +4 on his owner's initiative roll, wherever he is | R27 |
   | Shorty | +4 on both sabotage rolls if present at the target's system | R98 |
   | JB | while in any Port (aboard a ship or on his own), every reputation loss of his owner is 1 smaller | R86 |
   | Silent | while in Port, all of his owner's assets at that system are immune to sabotage | R97 |
   | Trigger | +1 to intercept for the ship carrying him | R70–R71 |
   | Frank | +2 to Black Market rolls on the planet he's on | black market (doesn't exist yet) |
   | X | +5 weapons for the ship carrying him | R75 |
   | Limpy | while in Port, his owner may act as Dictator for one good traded at that system, if no other player is its Dictator | R37–R38 |

2. **Badger and Frank** depend on planet landing and the black market, which don't exist yet (R31 gives the planet numbers
   only). They stay **out of the pool** until those features are built, so nobody can recruit an agent that does nothing.
   The pool is eight for now.

### Recruiting
3. **When:** on the player's Opportunity turn, after their Collect. **Once per Opportunity.** Refused before paying if the
   pool is empty: `No agents are left to recruit.`
4. **Paying:** the player chooses an amount from 10 to 60 HT and pays it up front. The form shows the chance of success
   (2d6 × 5 at or below the amount: 10 HT → 1/36, 35 HT → 21/36, 60 HT → certain). Paying more than 60 isn't offered.
5. **The roll:** the server rolls 2d6 × 5. If the result is at or below the amount paid, a **random** agent from the pool
   joins the player. Otherwise the money is lost and nobody joins. The payment, the roll and the agent's identity are
   private. The public log says only `Vega Lines recruited an agent at Tau Ceti`, and a failed attempt isn't logged.
6. **Where the agent starts:** the player chooses aboard one of their ships (not one in the yard) or on its own in the Port or
   on the Planet of a system where they have a ship or a warehouse.

### Where agents are, and who knows
7. **Positions:** an agent is aboard one of its owner's ships, or on its own in a Port or on a Planet, never on its own in
   Space. It takes no cargo space and travels with its ship on every jump.
8. **Public:** where every agent is, but not which agent it is. That means how many agents each player has on their own at
   each Port and Planet, and how many ride **each ship**, with the ship named (creator, 2026-11-20). It appears on the
   map's system panel, the Corporations card, and wherever a ship is listed. For example:
   `Vega Lines: 1 agent in Port at Tau Ceti` and `Starfarer (Vega Lines): 2 agents aboard`.
9. **Private to the owner:** each agent's name and effect, on a new **Agents** card on
   Infrastructure and on each ship's card on Fleet.
10. **No identities are tracked:** public views and the API give agents no stable id or name for other players, only counts
    per place and per ship (A05). Others learn who an agent was only from the log line when it acts. If two of a player's agents later
    stand together, nothing tells them apart.

### Acting reveals the agent
11. Each time an agent's effect applies, one public line names it and its owner:
    - `Novak (Vega Lines): +4 to Starfarer's jump`
    - `Silent (Orion Freight) foiled a sabotage at Tau Ceti`
    - `JB (Kessler Cargo): reputation loss 6 → 5`

    The line belongs to the event it changed: the jump, the initiative roll, the battle salvo, the sabotage or the trade.

### Moving agents
12. **On the owner's Opportunity turn**, and only then:
    - an agent on its own **boards** one of its owner's ships at the same system and location (Port with Port, Planet with
      Planet);
    - an agent aboard a ship in Port or on a Planet **steps off** there;
    - an agent moves **between two of its owner's ships** at the same system and location.

    Any number of moves per turn. The public counts (criterion 8) change with them, so everyone sees that an agent
    boarded or left a ship, though not which one.
13. Agents never move on their own between systems: only ships carry them.

### Selling agents
14. **On the seller's Opportunity turn**, they may offer one of their agents to another player for a price. The buyer accepts
    or declines from a notification at any time before Opportunity closes, after which the offer expires. Before accepting,
    the buyer sees only where the agent is, not who it is. A seller may tell them in chat.
15. **On accepting:** the buyer pays and the agent becomes theirs, and they learn who it is. An agent aboard the seller's ship
    steps off at the ship's location first. Agents aboard a ship in Space can't be offered, since an agent can't be on its
    own in Space. The public log: `Vega Lines sold an agent at Tau Ceti to Orion Freight`. The price is private (R29).
    Refused if the buyer can't pay.

### Losing agents
16. **Ship destroyed in Space** (battle, R77): every agent aboard is **killed** and leaves the game for good, never returning
    to the pool. The log says `2 agents aboard Starfarer were killed` without naming them.
17. **Ship destroyed elsewhere** (sabotage in Port, R99): the agents step off into that Port and survive.
18. **Ship sold** (R66): its agents step off at its location. If the ship is in Space, they step off into that system's Port.

### Effect details
19. **Novak, Trigger, X:** apply to the ship carrying them, adding +4 to the jump result, +1 to intercept and +5 to weapons
    respectively. They don't apply to a ship they merely share a system with.
20. **Wolf:** applies to every initiative roll of his owner, from anywhere.
21. **Shorty:** applies to a sabotage whose target system he's present at, on both rolls, including the factory-size effect
    roll.
22. **JB:** while he's in any Port, **every** reputation loss of his owner is reduced by 1, whatever the reason. That covers
    interception tries, firing in battle, a market-position fall, criminal connections, unpaid loan interest, sabotage,
    being blamed, and any loss added later. A loss of 1 becomes 0, and the −8 when blamed becomes −7. Gains aren't
    affected, and each reduced loss is its own reveal (criterion 11).
23. **Silent:** while he's in Port at a system, his owner's ships in Port, warehouse and factories at that system can't be
    sabotaged. The saboteur can't know he's there, so the attempt is paid for and then fails before any roll: no effect and
    no reputation loss. Silent is revealed (criterion 11).
24. **Limpy:** while he's in Port at a system, when a good there resolves in Trading (R34) and no other player is Dictator of
    that good at that system, his owner may act as its Dictator for that resolution, with the full powers of R37–R38. This applies to **one good per turn**:
    the first they use it on. They're asked as a Dictator would be, `Act as Dictator (Limpy)` or `Pass`, and passing keeps
    the power for another good that turn. It gives no market position: the next resolution is normal.

## 📝 Proposed Solution

An `Agent` enum holds the ten agents and their effects. An `AgentPool` value holds who's left. A player's agents are a list of
`RecruitedAgent(agent, position)` on `Player`, where `position` is aboard a ship (by name) or on its own at system +
location. `Player` owns recruiting, moving, selling and stepping off. Each feature asks the owning player for its modifier:
`Player.jumpBonusFor(ship)`, `initiativeBonus()`, `interceptBonusFor(ship)`, `weaponsBonusFor(ship)`,
`sabotageBonusAt(system)`, `isImmuneToSabotageAt(system)`, `reputationLossReduction()`, `mayDictateAt(system)`. Each reports
the acting agent so the event writes the reveal line. `Game` coordinates killing agents aboard a ship destroyed in battle.

**Alternatives considered:**
- *Public agent tokens with stable ids:* rejected, because the creator wants identities to blur when agents meet.
- *Keeping Badger and Frank in the pool:* rejected until their features exist.

## 📝 Architecture and data model

- `game/agent/` (new package): `Agent` (enum), `AgentPool`, `RecruitedAgent`, `AgentPosition` (sealed: `Aboard(shipName)`,
  `OnOwn(system, location)`), `AgentOffer`.
- `Game.agentPool`; `Player.agents`; `Turn.recruitedBy`, `Turn.agentOffers`, both emptied with Opportunity.
- The battle's destruction path kills agents aboard. The sale and sabotage-destruction paths step them off.
- Web: `POST /api/games/{id}/agents/recruit` (`amount`, `start`), `…/agents/move`, `…/agents/offer`, `…/agents/offers/{id}/accept|decline`.
  `PlayerView` carries the viewer's own agents with names. The public view carries only counts per player per place, with
  no ids.
- DynamoDB: new attributes, no migration.
- Frontend: an Agents card on Infrastructure (recruit with odds, own agents and moves, sell, incoming offers), agents on
  Fleet ship cards, counts on the system panel and Corporations, and offer notifications.

## 📝 Edge Cases

- **Two agents with the same effect type aboard one ship:** impossible, because each agent is unique.
- **Novak's ship jumps and is then destroyed in Space:** Novak acted (revealed), then dies.
- **Wolf's owner bids for initiative while Wolf is aboard a ship destroyed later that turn:** the +4 already applied.
- **Limpy at a system where his owner is already Dictator of some good:** that good doesn't need Limpy, and he applies to
  another good.
- **An agent's owner leaves Port** (all their ships jump away) with the agent on its own: it stays in Port at that system
  until a ship of theirs comes back.
- **The last turn:** recruiting still works, but nothing comes of it.

## Defaults I chose (override any)

- Recruiting is **once per Opportunity**, with **10–60 HT** paid, and a failed attempt **isn't logged**.
- A new agent starts **aboard a ship, or on its own where the player has a ship or warehouse**.
- Public counts are kept **per place and per ship**. Identities are never shown.
- Agents move **only on their owner's Opportunity turn**.
- **Selling:** the buyer doesn't see the identity before buying, and the offer expires when Opportunity closes.
- **Killed agents are gone for good.** In other destructions and sales, agents step off and survive.
- **Silent** covers ships in Port, the warehouse and the factories at that system.
- **Limpy** gives one good per turn, with full Dictator powers for that resolution, and no market position.
- **Badger and Frank are out of the pool** until planet landing and the black market exist.
