# Execution plan: E04-S03 — After each jump the player chooses where the ship ends up

Source doc: .ai/specs/2026-10-29-e04-ship-jumps-phase.md
Issue: #44
Stacked on: #83 (E04-S02), base branch `feat/e04-s02-ship-jumps` until #83 merges

## Goal

After each jump, the jumping player chooses where the ship ends up: Port, Planet or Space (R30). A modal dialog asks,
with Port pre-selected and Escape meaning "Stay in space". Until they confirm, the jump isn't finished and the game
waits (D09); a reload reopens the dialog. The choice is logged and shown in Your fleet and on s02.

## Scope

- `game` domain: `Turn.landingShip` (the ship that jumped and hasn't landed yet, or null; public). `Game.jumpShip` sets
  it and no longer ends the slot on the last ship; `Game.landShip(email, location)` places the ship, logs it (S03-3),
  clears it and ends the slot if that was the player's last ship (S03-4). Jumping again or finishing while a ship
  waits to land is refused (S02-3, S03-6).
- `ShipJumpsService.land` inside `GameStore.update` (A04).
- DynamoDB: `TurnItem.landingShip` (S, or absent); older items read as none.
- Web: `POST /api/games/{id}/ship-jumps/landing` `{ location }` → `200` + the game; `turn.landingShip` in the game.
- `static/js/landing.js` (new, pure, unit-tested): the dialog's heading and options with the R31 stats, whether the
  dialog is open for the viewer.
- `static/index.html`: s05 as a native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` (focus inside), Port pre-selected,
  **Confirm**, Escape = Stay in space; focus returns to "Your next jump" or "Your jumps are done." (S03-4).
- `BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md` §6 and §7.

## Non-goals

- Location mechanics (R31): the choice changes where the ship is shown, nothing else.
- Moving between Port, Planet and Space without jumping (S02-4, proposed: not in v1).
- S04's live-progress story beyond what polling already redraws.

## Decisions

- Base: stacked on `feat/e04-s02-ship-jumps` (#83 not merged yet); the PR retargets to `main` once #83 lands.
- **The waiting ship is stored, not derived**: `landingShip` names it. A ship that jumped but hasn't landed is in
  Space meanwhile (S02's default), which is also what Escape picks.
- **Slot end moves to the landing**: jumping the last ship no longer ends the slot; landing it does (S03-4). S02-6's
  behaviour is unchanged from the player's side.
- **Refusals**: no ship waiting → `409` "No ship is waiting to land."; jump or finish while one waits → `409` "Choose
  where {ship} ends up first."; a missing or unknown location → `400` "Choose where the ship ends up."; out of turn →
  `409` "It's {corp}'s turn to jump." (as S02).
- **Log lines** (S03-3): "Landing: {corp} — {ship} lands at the port ({system})", "… lands on the planet ({system})",
  "… stays in space ({system})".

## Risks

- `risk-medium`: a new stored attribute, a new endpoint, and S02's slot-end rule moves. Covered by domain tests
  (waiting state, refusals, slot end on landing, logs), round-trip and older-item tests, controller tests, a replayed
  update, JS unit tests and a browser walk (reload reopens the dialog, Escape).
