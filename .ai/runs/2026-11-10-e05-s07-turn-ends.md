# Execution plan: E05-S07 — The turn ends and the next turn starts with Bidding

Source doc: .ai/specs/2026-10-29-e05-trading-phase.md
Issue: #52

## Goal

When the last good of a turn has finished resolving (queue empty, nothing pending), s03 says "All goods resolved. The
next turn starts with Bidding." (S07-1). Unless it was the last turn the creator chose (R39, R41: `Game.turns`,
default 20), the turn number goes up and the phase becomes Bidding; offers that didn't trade expire (S07-2, proposed).
The new turn starts its own group in the log (S07-3).

## What already works (checked before planning)

- E05-S01 to S05 are merged (PRs #88–#92). A good counts as resolved once its Dictator, every trader and every
  wholesaler have answered (`Resolution.isFinished`), and `Turn.resolvedGoods` / `tradingQueue` track the turn.
- `Game.advancePhase` already maps Trading → `Turn.next()` (a fresh `Turn.bidding(n + 1)`, so no offers) or to
  `GameStatus.FINISHED` after `turns`, but only tests call it for Trading. In a real game nothing ever left Trading: after
  the last good the queue was empty, `resolver()` was null and s03 said "All 18 goods resolved." for ever.
- The game's length is `Game.turns`, set when the creator makes the game (1–50, the web form defaults to 20) and
  persisted on the game item. R39's "turn 20" is that number, not a constant.
- The log groups by `LogEntry.turn` (`js/log.js`), so a new group appears as soon as a line is logged under the new turn.
- Prices live on the game (`MarketPrices`, E05-S05), so they carry over by themselves (R35).

## Scope

- **Domain** (`game`): `Turn.isEveryGoodResolved()`; `Game.endTurn(current)`, the one place a turn ends: it logs "All
  goods resolved" in the ending turn, then either finishes the game (last turn) or starts `Turn.next()` and logs "Turn
  {n} of {N} starts with Bidding; turn {n-1}'s offers have expired" under the new turn. `Game.withResolutionMoved`, which
  every resolution action already goes through, calls it in the same step that finishes the last good;
  `advancePhase`'s Trading branch calls it too.
- **Web**: no new field or endpoint; the response simply shows the next turn.
- **Frontend**: `tradingStatus(game, email)` in `js/trading.js` gives s03's status line: during Trading whom the game
  waits for, in the Bidding of a turn after the first "All goods resolved. The next turn starts with Bidding.", else
  "Trading starts after Ship-jumps…". Focus goes to that status after the action that ended the turn.

## Non-goals

- The game-over screen, final money and winner (E05-S08), beyond keeping the existing "finished after the last turn".
- Position rolls and their "from the next turn" changes (E05-S06, built in parallel). `Game.endTurn` is where they plug in.
- Deleting the old `trading` package.

## Decisions (autonomous, reversible)

- **The turn ends by itself, in the same update as the answer that finishes the last good.** No button, no timer (D09):
  Bidding and Ship-jumps also close the moment the last player is through. Doing it inside the same `GameStore.update`
  makes it atomic (standard §9): a double press can't start two turns.
- **One method for the turn's end, `Game.endTurn`.** It's private, called from `withResolutionMoved` and `advancePhase`.
  E05-S06's pending position changes apply there, before the next turn's Bidding.
- **s03 shows the S07-1 text through the next turn's Bidding**, because the server never sits in "Trading, all
  resolved": the moment the last good resolves, the game is in turn n+1's Bidding. The last good's result stays in
  the log (and the progress announcer reads the last lines out to the others).
- **Two log lines**: "All goods resolved" in the turn that ended, then "Turn 5 of 20 starts with Bidding; turn 4's
  offers have expired" in the new one, which starts the new group (S07-3) and tells players their offers are gone
  (S07-2's "expire", proposed). It doesn't name any offer (R17).
- **A game saved after its last good resolved but before this change stays in Trading.** The project is pre-launch
  (BACKWARD_COMPATIBILITY.md), so no migration.

## Implementation Plan

### Phase 1: Domain

1. `Turn.isEveryGoodResolved`, `Game.endTurn`, called from `withResolutionMoved` and `advancePhase`. Tests:
   `GameTurnEndTest` (last trade ends the turn, last good with no offers, offers expire, log lines, prices carry over,
   one-turn game finishes).

### Phase 2: Adapters

1. `GameControllerTest`: resolving all 18 goods over HTTP leaves the game in turn 2's Bidding, saved, with the log
   lines; Resolve next is then refused.

### Phase 3: Frontend

1. `tradingStatus` in `js/trading.js` with tests; s03 uses it.

### Phase 4: Verify

1. Full gate (`./gradlew build`), then a browser walk with two players: the last good ended by Resolve next and by a
   trade, both players' s03, the log's new group, the Deck's empty offers, phone width.

## Risks

- Every resolution action can now end the turn. They all go through `withResolutionMoved`, so there's one call site.
- A test that walks a whole Trading phase now ends in the next turn's Bidding instead of an empty queue. None did.

## Notes from the run

- **Changed while building:** after the trade that ends the turn, focus went to `<body>`: `sendTrade` and the Dictator's
  decision focused `#resolution-title`, which is hidden once the turn has moved on. Both now focus the status line when
  the resolution panel is hidden, as Resolve next already did (2d39804).
- **Docker was running**, so `GameControllerTest` and the DynamoDB tests ran: 509 Kotlin tests, 0 skipped; 190 JS tests.
- **Browser walk** (agent-browser 0.35.2, two players, own DynamoDB Local on 8170 and app on 3170 with
  `RESEND_API_KEY=` blank; game set up and 17 goods resolved through the API, Orion's buy of Tau Ceti POLY at 20 HT
  put last):
  - Vega (first in initiative) pressed Resolve next on s03 ("17 of 18 resolved"). Orion's s03 showed his amount form.
    He bought 5 into Halcyon. His s03 went straight to "All goods resolved. The next turn starts with Bidding.", the top
    bar to "Turn 2 of 20 · Bidding — waiting for you, Vega Lines", money 300 → 200 HT. Vega's s03 showed the same after
    her next poll, and her live region read "POLY price at Tau Ceti: 6 → 8 HT. All goods resolved. Turn 2 of 20 starts
    with Bidding; turn 1's offers have expired."
  - The Game log had a new "Turn 2" group above "Turn 1", holding "Turn 2 of 20 starts with Bidding; turn 1's offers
    have expired". Orion's Deck said "No offers yet this turn."
  - Turn 2 was driven the same way; at 390 px Orion's trade ended it. s03 read the S07-1 text, the top bar "Turn 3 of 20
    · Bidding", focus was on the status line (after the fix above), and there was no horizontal scroll.
