# References: Star Trader

Date: 2026-10-27. Selected by: project creator (the owner of this repo).

**Character words (confirmed):** control-deck, instrumented, luminous, legible, spatial.

**Anti-character (confirmed):** generic, dry, off-game, ornamental, spreadsheet-like.

Sources opened: `design/mockups/{ships,factories,index}.html` (rendered at 1440 px, plus `trade-offers.html` read in code and screenshot), `src/main/resources/static/index.html` rendered live at `localhost:3000/`, `.ai/specs/product-brief.md`.
Limit: Google Fonts were blocked in my render, so typography observations come from the declared CSS (Orbitron for display, Rajdhani for body), not from seeing the faces on screen.

## Gaps

Families with no reference:
- **Motion and response:** no reference. Decided so far: no real time, no ambient animation, a slight glow on hover is fine (A04, P11). Selection (a node, ship or row) is shown by a highlight or a simple glow-like transition (creator's answer). A value changed by a poll (a price, a ship arriving) flashes briefly (creator's answer).

Unanswered questions:
1. **S/D scale details:** the S/D colour shows on the star system screen, from green to red by buy chance (my calculation in R03.5, to confirm). Stepped or a smooth blend, what it means for a seller, and the exact colors will be decided by looking at mockups, not in words.
2. **Info visible about other players' ships:** only the ship and its owner for now; the scope of what else is visible (cargo, etc.) will be decided later.
3. **Goods colors:** chosen in principle (R04.1). Exact shades, and whether lime FOOD and dark-blue POLY read well next to green/red and on navy, are to be checked on the mockups.
4. **Numbers on a node and edge colors (later):** the other numbers shown on a node, and the "ease of jump" scale, will be provided later. In v1 every jump is instant to anywhere (R19/R20).
5. **Mockups to come:** the graph dashboard, the system screen and the combined warehouses+factories screen will be created later from these decisions. Nothing above rests on a mockup for them.
6. **A02 exceptions:** none listed yet. Any "default-looking" element you choose on purpose while browsing mockups gets recorded here.
